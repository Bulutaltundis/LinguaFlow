Refund Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for LinguaFlow subscriptions and paid services.

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation prevents future renewals. Unless otherwise required by applicable law, cancellation does not automatically refund a payment already processed. You will generally retain access until the end of the paid period.

2. Refund Requests

If you were charged incorrectly, experienced a significant billing problem, or believe you are otherwise entitled to a refund, contact us. Include your account email, approximate transaction date, subscription plan, and a description of the issue. We review requests case by case where permitted by applicable law.

3. Accidental or Duplicate Charges

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same period or charged due to a technical billing error, contact us as soon as possible. We will investigate and, where appropriate, arrange a correction or refund.

4. Free Trials

If LinguaFlow offers a free trial, the applicable trial terms will be displayed before you start it. If the trial converts into a paid subscription, the presented price will be charged according to the signup terms.

5. Legal Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy limits mandatory refund, cancellation, withdrawal, or consumer rights that apply to you under applicable law.

6. Payment Provider

Payments may be processed through Paddle. Depending on the transaction and applicable arrangements, Paddle may handle payment processing, refunds, and related billing operations.
